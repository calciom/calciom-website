Project Architecture & Design Choices

Welcome to Calciom. This dedicated portal houses the core architectural principles, design patterns, and engineering choices behind the framework.

  • Open Source & Community Driven: Developed under independent governance to foster collaboration and expansion.
  • C Native Execution: Engineered entirely around the performance and minimalism of standard C.
  • Source & Documentation: Complete technical code references are directly embedded in the repository codebase.
